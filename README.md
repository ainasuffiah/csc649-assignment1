# Web-Based Dynamic Queue Optimization System Using Priority Scheduling

## Project Overview
This project explores dynamic queue optimization using weighted priority scheduling for general outpatient consultation at government clinics in Malaysia. The main factors considered are patients' priority and waiting time.

## Problem Statement
Managing patient queues in government clinics can be challenging, especially during busy periods. Long waiting times and different patient priority levels may affect patient satisfaction and clinic efficiency. A study by Shalihin et al. (2020) reported an average total waiting time of 33.23 minutes for diabetic patients to see a doctor at a government clinic in Kuantan, Pahang. This issue affects patients who are waiting for consultation and clinic staff who need to manage the queue. Therefore, a more dynamic scheduling approach could help clinic staff manage patient queues more effectively.

## Proposed Technology
- Category: Scheduling and Optimization Algorithms
- Main Algorithm: Dynamic Weighted Priority Scheduling
- Alternative Approach: First Come First Served (FCFS)

# Part A: Problem Formulation

## A1. Problem Statement

Managing patient queues in government clinics can be challenging, especially during busy periods. Long waiting times and different patient priority levels may affect patient satisfaction and clinic efficiency. A study by Shalihin et al. (2020) reported an average total waiting time of 33.23 minutes for diabetic patients to see a doctor at a government clinic in Kuantan, Pahang. This issue affects patients who are waiting for consultation and clinic staff who need to manage the queue. Therefore, a more dynamic scheduling approach could help clinic staff manage patient queues more effectively.

## A2. Users and Stakeholders

### Main Users

1. **Patients**
   - Scan a QR code and fill in their registration details.
   - Receive a queue number and check their queue status.
   - View their estimated waiting time.

2. **Clinic Staff**
   - Monitor and manage patient queues through a dashboard.
   - Verify patient information and priority levels.
   - Monitor doctor availability and update patient status.
   - Call the next patient for consultation.

### Stakeholders

1. **Doctors**
   - Provide consultations to patients assigned through the queue system.

2. **Clinic Management**
   - Oversee clinic operations and monitor queue management efficiency.

## Project Scope

### In Scope (Included)

- Focus on general outpatient consultation services at government health clinics (Klinik Kesihatan).
- Web-based system for patients and clinic staff.
- Patient self-registration by scanning a QR code.
- Patient queue prioritisation based on priority level and waiting time using weighted priority scheduling.
- Automatic queue updates when patient status or waiting time changes.
- Generate queue number and monitor status for patients.
- Clinic staff dashboard to manage patient queues and monitor doctor availability.
- Use of dummy patient data for system testing and evaluation.
- Estimated waiting time display for patients.

### Out of Scope (Excluded)

- Emergency treatment and medical diagnosis.
- Pharmacy, laboratory, radiology, and specialist clinic services.
- Integration with real hospital or clinic systems.
- Use of real patient data.
- Payment and billing processes.
